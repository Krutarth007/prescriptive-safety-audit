# Draft results narrative

Across 36069 MIMIC-IV and 86403 eICU-CRD landmark-eligible ICU stays, calibrated models discriminated in-hospital mortality with an internal AUROC of about 0.91 and transported to eICU with the expected modest degradation (Table 2, Table 4). Treatment was strongly confounded by indication; after a dedicated logistic overlap-weighting the maximum standardised mean difference across covariates fell from 1.52 to 0.00 (fig_love_balance), defining the equipoise population in which a de-escalation decision is faced (fig_propensity_overlap). The doubly robust effect of organ support on the overlap population was +0.013 (Table 5, E-value 2.96); the confidence interval includes zero and the sign is consistent with residual confounding, so we do not interpret it as a causal effect.

The treatment-effect model on which any prescriptive verdict rests did not discriminate individual benefit (c-for-benefit 0.57; fig_benefit_validation). To determine whether this makes the audit unreliable, we built a semi-synthetic benchmark with a known benefit and a fixed true Deployment Hazard Index, and degraded only the covariate information available to the treatment-effect model (fig_audit_operating_characteristic). The audit recovered the true hazard without systematic bias only above a c-for-benefit floor of approximately 0.63; below that floor the estimated hazard collapsed toward zero while the true hazard remained large. Both ICU cohorts fell below the floor (c-for-benefit 0.55 internal, 0.47 external), so the deployment-hazard numbers computed on MIMIC and eICU are within the audit's blind zone and are reported descriptively rather than as evidence of harm. Consistent with this, the withdrawal-arm hazard is at or below zero at baseline, so no benefit bias is required to nullify it and the headline hazard is entirely an escalation-arm phenomenon; the withdrawal mechanism is not operative here, the top risk and treatment drivers did not overlap (0 of 15), and negative-control outcomes immutable to treatment returned near-zero adjusted effects (fig_negative_controls).
